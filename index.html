Tencent (Level Infinite) · London · 2026

Self-serve SDK integration for game studios

Integration turnaround cut from 3–4 days to half a day.

  • Co-built a Claude Code and Codex plugin that turns a plain-language request into validated, version-matched integration code for Level Infinite PASS, Tencent's global player-account platform.
  • Owned its hardest skill, login compliance. It detects the project's SDK version, retrieves the matching docs, samples and source, and pulls compliance rules from the server, so a new region needs no plugin change.
  • Guardrails stop the agent on missing or conflicting evidence instead of letting it guess an API. Formal QA hardened it, including concurrency fixes.
  • During rollout I worked inside the game teams' channels across Unity and Unreal Engine builds, fixing bugs as they came in.

Live: Roco Kingdom: World, Honor of Kings Chess, Crownstone Survival. Onboarding: Delta Force, NIKKE.

Coach Cube AI · London · 2025

Real-time 3D motion tracking, from zero to investor demo

Delivered in 8 weeks as the only computer-vision engineer on a three-person team.

  • Three synchronized 720p cameras, YOLO11 pose on GPU in FP16, multi-view DLT triangulation with single-view fallback, and per-joint Kalman filtering across 17 joints.
  • Chose the models through telemetry-driven bake-offs of YOLOv8, YOLO11 and VideoPose3D on accuracy, latency and compute cost.
  • Streamed joints to Unity over WebSocket at about 30 Hz behind a stable output contract. Shipped in Docker with CI/CD.

How I work

Five habits, and where they showed up.

  • Sit with the people using it.

    During rollout I worked inside the game teams' channels and fixed bugs as they landed, so integration cost them as little time as possible.

  • Ship complete, with dates.

    I pushed to test every platform before handing the plugin over, and backed it with a firm timeline. My lead agreed.

  • Measure, then choose.

    Coach Cube's models were picked by bake-offs on accuracy, latency and compute cost.

  • Never let an agent grade itself.

    When a coding agent rewrote its own grader, I reverted the change and locked the rules.

  • Ramp fast.

    I went from zero SDK knowledge to owning the plugin's hardest skill in about a month, while finishing my MSc thesis.
